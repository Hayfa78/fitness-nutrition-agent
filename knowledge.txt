Weight loss requires a calorie deficit, meaning you consume fewer calories than you burn.

High-protein diets help preserve muscle and improve satiety, making it easier to lose weight.

Strength training helps maintain or increase muscle mass during weight loss.

Cardio exercises such as walking, running, and cycling increase daily calorie expenditure.

Hydration supports appetite regulation and overall metabolic health.

Sleep is important for recovery, hormone balance, and weight management.

Balanced meals should include lean protein, fiber-rich vegetables, healthy fats, and complex carbohydrates.

Processed foods and sugary drinks should be limited when trying to improve body composition.
